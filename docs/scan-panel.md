# Scan controls

The left column contains the camera views and Pop out. On the right, **Sides** contains saved side cards, New side and Bed learn. **Settings** contains Capture, Object, framing, Range, IR gain, Light, Save color, Cut below bed, Remove base and Camera setup. Start, Pause and Build stay below both tabs. Keyboard shortcuts work on either tab.

The chosen tab stays for the session. A new session starts on Sides. A missing calibration can open Settings once to show the fix; state updates do not keep taking the tab away, and a slider drag is never interrupted. A routine camera check does not open calibration folders.

Framing has **Use part Range** and **Undo**. The box follows the measured part and grows to the full bounds and depth span seen during this side. A new side or a large scene move resets that span. Use part Range rounds the turn-wide span outward to the existing Range step; Undo restores the previous cut and Auto setting. Capture must be paused. The status line gives one hint; hover there for point counts, fill, available dot counts and setup details. Its explanation is not attached to the camera picture or model panel. Gain details and the color-storage estimate are on hover too.

The part outline contains the full supported, connected above-bed depth, including thin overhangs. Small isolated components and below-bed noise stay excluded. Color bounds include the measured depth span so camera parallax cannot cut off a tip. The faint border marks the depth view at the part distance. It is a guide at that distance, not a measurement of invisible surfaces.

The plain plus is the scanner aim. **Show** controls depth dots, the part box, aim, part center, depth edge, left IR view and right IR view. Dots, box and aim start on; center and edges start off. These choices are saved per user and shared with the big color view and pop-out. The optional center dot has a line to the aim. Within a tenth of the box, it becomes a ring around the aim. Box color carries the framing state. No framing text covers the picture, and no edge dims the image outside it.

The box, aim, center line and edges are drawn in Qt after image scaling, with light over dark strokes sized by the screen font. Depth dots remain a raster layer. The depth edge is the convex envelope of recent valid depth near the part and its measured bed, not the sensor rectangle or a promise of optical coverage where no depth has ever appeared. Interior gaps are kept separate from the edge. The part defining its own envelope is not proof of clipping; an outside-view hint needs a sensor boundary or independent background support at both ends of a contour segment. Substantial holes inside observed part support get a missing-depth hint. A large enclosed dark gap touching part depth gets a cautious cue to check dark or shiny areas. If an edge and an interior gap are both present, the status reports both. A completely unseen extension cannot be reliably identified from IR alone. The guide does not invent its shape or alter saved depth.

The **Left / Right / Both** selector switches IR cameras. Right uses the supplied rectified stereo geometry for the aim and depth edge. Both blends cyan left and warm right in left-camera coordinates, using per-pixel depth where present and the part plane elsewhere. Plane regions can retain parallax. There is no wider stitching. Old clips without right IR show an empty right view and a message outside the picture. New kept frames save optional raw `.irr` files: 480,000 bytes per 800x600 frame, one third more than depth plus left IR. Back, Forward, replay and calibration-copy restoration keep the right file with its frame.

The small ring shows views retained around the learned bed, with degrees turned. Each slice needs a kept, tracked frame at that angle; gaps are not interpolated. Its color is the best shared distance band reached there. Gray means a kept view without saved distance evidence. This measures angular capture coverage, not complete surface coverage. The disposable `turn-coverage.json` is saved with the side and rebuilt from retained poses after Back or Forward. Old frames without an explicit camera mode do not get a guessed distance band.

The distance bar keeps its own zone colors and measured-data limits. Overlapping bands are resolved before labels are placed. The center marker starts below the labels. Part distance is bold on the left, Range is on the right, and text is shortened on narrower views rather than overlapping. Color alone does not claim physical accuracy or an optical working-distance limit.

A confirmed camera match survives checks with too few visible dots or failed color evidence. Only a measured scene change or repeated contrary votes invalidates it. The last confirmed scene reference is preserved while evidence is unclear, so a series of small moves cannot hide a large cumulative move. Held results are recorded in Log without replacing the confirmed UI notice.

Light failures before a stream is present do not show as a persistent device failure. The first complete depth frame retries the last requested light state once. Late replies from before reconnect are ignored. A failure from a command requested with the device present still shows as failed. Only verified readback can show that the light is on.

Auto range uses the whole connected part, with three times the measured depth noise as margin and outward rounding to the existing Range step. It expands immediately and waits for the existing hold before narrowing. Depth that Range removes remains visible as dim distance-colored dots in Color, so a tight cut is visible before recording.

The distance bar uses contiguous bands, matching the colors on the depth dots and the Capture distance model view. With no saved Range test, the fallback is the observed span of saved scans: its middle 40% is Best, 30% on either side is OK, and outside it is off. This is a display rule, not an accuracy rating. The axis stays fixed for the mode: the supported span plus half that span on each side, clipped to the slider limits. Moving the part or Range does not rescale it. Equalizer columns split source histogram counts by overlap, with no sampling alias. Millimeter ticks show the scale. The bottom line says Good distance or how far to move toward Best, followed by a framing hint when needed.

A camera mismatch puts a **Use Small part** or **Use Large part** button in a reserved row above the camera image. Camera setup also has **Auto align**, small horizontal, vertical, rotation and scale steps, and **Reset overlay**. Good dot matches can refine the color overlay within the observed dot radius; the log and setup show the error before and after. A contrary camera match prevents fine-tuning. Manual values are saved per calibration fingerprint. Reset removes both the session fit and manual values. Alignment moves only the color overlay, never depth, tracking, fusion or saved poses.

**Range test** is for an empty learned dot mat. Choose the camera on the scanner before PC mode, choose its Object setting, press Range test, and move slowly from near to far. Stop and save when done. The live hint asks you to slow down while analysis catches up. The chart and hover details show distance-bin fill, flatness RMS, dot count and fit, overlay error, and both camera matches. At least three observed distance bins and a confirmed matching camera are required to replace a profile. Best is the contiguous run with fill at least the median and noise at most the median, ranked by supporting frames. Gaps remain unmeasured. Date, calibration identity, raw evidence and the rule are saved in config. Reset test restores the shipped profile. Short held clips are partial evidence, not a full working-distance sweep. Fewer than three bins show: Not enough distances yet, keep moving.

**Capture distance** is the default model view when evidence is available. Each tracker-size voxel keeps the best distance band in which it was captured. Better evidence upgrades it; a worse later view cannot downgrade it. Red is off, yellow is OK, green is Best, and gray has no saved evidence. Switch to Frame support or Shaded when needed. The latest colored model stays visible while its next update is measured. Recording saves the band policy with each frame. A separate lower-priority process reads the saved depth and poses to build the disposable per-side voxel field; it adds no tracking or fusion operation. Undo rebuilds the evidence from the retained frames. Build's color menu and Touch up can show the same evidence. Old frames with a saved mode can be reconstructed from their saved distances using the shipped profile. Saved per-frame bands take priority. No camera mode is guessed. A reconstructed surface can use saved support within one voxel diagonal, the combined sampling bound; farther gaps remain gray. Build meshes query each facet center. The key wraps to keep all entries visible, and Capture distance uses the exact shared RGB values without lighting changes.

Periodic late arrivals with full valid depth are recorded in Log without a depth-loss warning. Repeating near-empty depth still warns.

The camera mismatch action has its own row above the image so it cannot cover the part box or its label. Camera setup groups the overlay nudges and shows their step size. The displayed dot error compares the original fit with the current auto plus manual transform, so a manual adjustment can make the error better or worse. Depth and poses are unchanged.
