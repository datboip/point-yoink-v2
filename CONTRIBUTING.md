# Contributing to PointYoink

Bug reports, test results and ideas are very welcome, especially from MIRACO and MIRACO Plus owners and from PCs other than the maintainer's.

## What helps most

- **A bug report.** Use **Help → Report a problem** in the app: it collects the log for you. Then open an issue and paste it.
- **Test results.** Which scanner, which PC, what worked and what did not.
- **Ideas.** Say what you were trying to do and where it got in your way.
